Frequently Asked Questions

What is the eligibility for Vihaan X? ✦

Vihaan X is open to all enrolled college undergraduate and postgraduate students from any recognized university across India and abroad. Beginners and experienced hackers are equally welcomed!

Is there any registration fee? ✦

Zero, nada! Vihaan X is completely free of cost for all shortlisted hackers thanks to our generous IEEE sponsors and DTU alumni support. Food, swag, internet, and accommodation are 100% on us.

What are the team size constraints? ✦

Teams can consist of 2 to 4 members. Inter-college and inter-disciplinary teams are strongly encouraged. Individual solo participation is not permitted to maximize collaborative brainstorming.

How does the Hybrid mode operate? ✦

Shortlisted teams residing in Delhi NCR attend in-person at the prestigious DTU Campus in New Delhi. Outstation teams have the flexibility to hack offline on campus (accommodations provided) or pitch via the virtual metaverse track.
